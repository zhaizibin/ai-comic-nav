# Example: Competitive Strategy Analysis

Prompt:

```text
Use Deep Analysis to compare the competitive positions of two products in the same market segment. Separate public claims from financial data, identify incentive asymmetries, model likely competitive dynamics, and estimate market-share outcomes over 12 months.
```

Expected workflow:

1. **Layered Information** — Separate public claims (marketing, press releases), reported financial data, and third-party analyst interpretations.
2. **Anomaly-First** — Identify unexpected strategic moves (price cuts, sudden market expansion, partnership shifts).
3. **Interest Structure** — Map stakeholder incentives: customers, suppliers, regulators, investors.
4. **Causal Mechanism** — Trace how the competitive advantage (or disadvantage) was built.
5. **Cross-Domain** — Apply patterns from analogous market battles in adjacent segments.
6. **Game-Theory** — Model the Nash equilibrium and identify instability triggers.
7. **Probability Anchoring** — Estimate market-share outcomes with confidence ranges.
8. **Devil's Advocate** — Stress-test the strongest assumption (e.g., "the incumbent cannot respond fast enough").
9. **Consistency Audit** — Verify no contradictions between the causal story and the probability estimates.

Expected output:

```
Bottom line
Evidence map
Key anomaly
Causal mechanism of competitive advantage
Stakeholder incentive map
Competing strategic hypotheses
Equilibrium analysis and instability triggers
Probability ranges for market-share outcomes
Strongest counterargument
What evidence would change the conclusion
```
