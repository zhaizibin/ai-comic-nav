# Example: Policy Analysis

Prompt:

```text
Use Deep Analysis to analyze whether a proposed data-governance policy is likely to be implemented within 12 months.
```

Expected workflow:

1. Separate official claims, stakeholder narratives, and verifiable facts.
2. Identify anomalies between stated goals and incentives.
3. Trace implementation mechanisms and institutional constraints.
4. Map stakeholders and their red lines.
5. Estimate probability with drivers and update triggers.
6. Run a counterargument and consistency audit.
