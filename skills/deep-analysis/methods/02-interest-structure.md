# Interest Structure Analysis

Use when the user asks who benefits, why a system persists, or what incentives sit beneath the stated narrative.

## Goal

Map incentives and constraints without inventing hidden intent. Treat motive claims as hypotheses that require support.

## Procedure

1. Identify actors and affected parties.
2. Separate stated goals from observable incentives.
3. Map benefits, costs, risks, and control points.
4. Ask which outcomes each actor can influence and which they cannot.
5. Generate competing incentive hypotheses.
6. Mark the evidence strength for each hypothesis.

## Guardrails

- Do not assert conspiracy or secret coordination without evidence.
- Prefer "this structure rewards X" over "they secretly wanted X" unless intent evidence exists.
- Include benign, accidental, and institutional explanations alongside strategic ones.
