# Probability Anchoring

Use when the answer involves likelihood, forecast, risk, success rate, or uncertain judgment.

## Goal

Prevent vague prediction by attaching a probability range and naming the drivers.

## Procedure

1. Convert the question into a falsifiable proposition with a time horizon.
2. List drivers that increase probability.
3. List drivers that decrease probability.
4. Estimate a range, not a fake-precise number.
5. Separate base rate, conditional probability, and scenario probability.
6. State what evidence would move the estimate.

## Output

```text
Proposition:
Time horizon:
Probability range:

Drivers up:
- ...

Drivers down:
- ...

Scenario table:
| Scenario | Condition | Probability |

Update triggers:
- Evidence that raises estimate:
- Evidence that lowers estimate:
```
