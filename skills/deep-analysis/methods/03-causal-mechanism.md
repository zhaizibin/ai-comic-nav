# Causal Mechanism Penetration

Use when the user asks why something happened, how it developed, or what mechanism transmitted cause into outcome.

## Goal

Move from surface description to causal chain, then to structural constraints.

## Procedure

1. State the exact outcome being explained.
2. List the conditions that had to be true for the outcome to occur.
3. For each condition, ask why it existed.
4. Distinguish proximate causes from structural causes.
5. Identify feedback loops, bottlenecks, incentive distortions, and information failures.
6. Stop only when the next "why" becomes generic or unsupported.

## Output

```text
Outcome:

Condition tree:
- Condition A
  - Proximate cause:
  - Deeper mechanism:
  - Structural constraint:

Most important mechanism:

What evidence would weaken this causal story:
```
